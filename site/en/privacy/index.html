Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

BrainForKids (B4K) (b4k.io) is a venture of Project Elijah, operated by Ronen Priewer in Israel. For any privacy question or request, write to privacy@projectelijah.io.

What we collect

We only receive what you choose to send us through the forms on this site:

  • Joining the declaration: your name, email and role (teacher, parent, school or other), so we can get back to you and keep you informed about the pilot.

We use these details only to reply to you and for the purpose of the form you filled in. We do not sell them, rent them or use them for advertising.

To understand how many people visit and which pages they read, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and does not follow you across sites. We see totals only: page views, the country, the referring site and the browser type.

Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Who handles the data

Netlify (hosting and form submissions) and Cloudflare (visit statistics). Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Form submissions are kept only as long as needed to answer you or to keep you informed about the venture. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you, under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation. Write to privacy@projectelijah.io and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
